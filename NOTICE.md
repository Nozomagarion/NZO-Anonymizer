# Third-party models and licences

The code in this repository is MIT. **The AI models it downloads are not included here and have their own licences:**

| Model | Used for | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| InsightFace `buffalo_l` (SCRFD det_10g, ArcFace w600k_r50, genderage) | detect, recognise and describe faces | InsightFace models: **non-commercial research use only** |
| `inswapper_128` | generate the replacement face | InsightFace: **non-commercial research use only** |
| GFPGAN 1.4 | optional HD restoration | Apache 2.0 |
| thispersondoesnotexist.com portraits | pool of AI faces (downloaded to your PC) | generated faces, see the site's terms |

If you want to use Anonymizer in a **commercial** project, get a commercial licence from InsightFace first, or replace those models with ones you are allowed to use.

Please use this tool to **protect** the people you film. Do not use face replacement to impersonate someone.
